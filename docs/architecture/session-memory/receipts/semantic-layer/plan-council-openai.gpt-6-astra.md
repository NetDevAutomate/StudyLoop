## Answers

**Q1.** §1 supports R1–R4, but “ONE service” is an architectural interpretation and `memory_search` is an explicit scope decision, not automatically covered by planner reuse. Add two checkable requirements: optional semantic dependencies must not break ordinary local DB maintenance; and deletion/scrubbing must invalidate derived data despite concurrent indexing. At close-out, distinguish a service named “semantic” from enabled, measured non-lexical retrieval. F1, F2 and F8 prevent false completion.

**Q2.** Agree with D1. §2.2 (`query_utils:180`, `mcp_server.py:78–90`) identifies unsafe FTS routing and the separate all-stop-word empty case. Embeddings do not fix malformed lexical `MATCH`, explicit-syntax handling or CLI/MCP divergence. Conversely, quoting tokens and widening AND→OR cannot recover a genuinely zero-overlap paraphrase. Stage 12 is the correct control, but compare a frozen paired arm rather than relying only on historical `0.1066`.

**Q3.** §2.3 supports local derived vectors and removing session-vector merge drift. It does not support “stale can never exist.” `_preserve_message_identity` can escape content-only invalidation when FK enforcement is absent; an embedding job can publish old content after scrub. Require both tests in F1. Also verify ordinary DB writers can execute vector-deleting triggers without semantic dependencies installed. `sqlite-vec` is a reasonable choice conditional on those checks, not yet a proven alignment mechanism.

**Q4.** §2.1 gives dimensions and mpnet timing, not comparative quality or all-model cost. For D3, measure the fraction of retrieval-relevant content lost beyond each tokenizer’s configured cap, rather than share above 2 KB. For D4, no single Stage 11 throughput measurement can select the best model: the decisive measurement should be confirmatory retrieval lift under the fixed latency/storage budget, obtained in Stage 14. Stage 11 throughput can reject infeasible candidates, not establish semantic superiority.

**Q5.** §2.4 provides complementary rulers: curated session relevance and broad self-retrieval. Keep both, but census is vulnerable to self-copy leakage and its ceiling needs an explicit oracle. “70% of ceiling” is stable only after corpus, visibility, equivalence groups and exclusions are frozen. The three hidden gold targets imply a pooled maximum of `88/91 ≈ 96.70%`; the **macro** maximum requires their stratum allocation. Missing guardrails include confirmatory holdout evaluation, numerical per-stratum regression margins and filtered-scope recall.

**Q6.** Integration is most underspecified for alignment: §4 describes a mostly serial lifecycle, whereas §2.3 exposes heterogeneous connections, rekeying and row-level sync. Insist on a paused-backfill-versus-scrub test: compute A, scrub to B, resume A, and assert A cannot commit. Add extension-unavailable writers, identity updates, transactional rollback, chunk deletion and metadata/`vec0` drift tests. Validation also needs the statistical definitions in F5–F7; deterministic fake vectors cannot establish real paraphrase quality.

**Q7.** Yes. §2.4 explicitly corrects the prior scorer: it reissued SQL rather than calling FastMCP. `call_tool` is the appropriate agent-facing gate, with exceptions, status and serialization included—not just returned IDs. It must be bound to an explicit control/treatment configuration and isolated test DB. Keep internal arms for diagnosis, and independently gate CLI parity because §2.5 documents the advertised fallback’s different behavior.

**Q8.** §1 says Stage 6 remains owner-gated; nothing supplied establishes that ontology DROP must precede embeddings. Prefer separate logical migrations and approvals, even within one backup/rehearsal window. Do not let an unrelated DROP block Stage 11 or the lexical fix. Folding retirement and close-out into Stage 15 is reasonable if their original acceptance obligations survive unchanged; those obligations are **UNVERIFIED** here. Extension/write-path and schema review must precede the migration, not merely review its aftermath.

**Q9.** §2.5 and D7 identify the risk: `session_search` and CLI might share code while `memory_search` only shares tokenization, skills still invoke bypasses, or the validated hybrid is disabled in the shipped configuration. Any of these makes an unqualified claim misleading. Check entry-point call graphs, installed defaults, ordered-result parity and runtime status. An off-by-default experiment is an honest outcome, but it does not fulfill R1-semantic without an explicit owner-approved deferral.

**Q10.** Nothing supplied blocks a genuinely read-only Stage 11. Before collecting its baseline, freeze question IDs, snapshot/scope, exclusions, arm commits and metric definitions; otherwise receipts will not support paired claims. Treat ceiling construction as an investigation with possible **UNVERIFIED** output, not a promised number. The major findings block later design/acceptance claims, especially migration approval and established-lift claims—not initial instrumentation. Owner approval for changed council cadence remains necessary before using it as the programme’s governance contract.
