# Stage24 council arbitration

The gateway preflight was healthy on 2026-09-06. Meta (`llama4-maverick`), Qwen
(`qwen3-coder`) and Mistral (`mistral-large-3`) all returned structured reviews of
one common brief, collection/selection code and twelve-case measurements. No real

The council did not approve production release. Neither does this checkpoint.
The full P01–P15 contract remains active.

## Accepted concerns and resulting decisions

Qwen and Mistral identified the strongest risk: an unverified relation label can
gain selection priority and displace useful context. A complete evidence group
is easier to inspect, but completeness does not establish its meaning. Keep the
explicit unverified status, preserve the strongest lexical anchor and require
review lineage/treatment of disputed interpretations as continuing P05 work.
This increment must not be presented as autonomous semantic arbitration.

Repeated proposals from different producers can amplify the same relation.
The final collector collapses exact endpoint/label duplicates before the discovery
limit. All records remain in storage; a stable representative is not a winning
vote. A dedicated regression adds 35 duplicate producers and checks that one
group remains, with no spurious discovery-limit signal. This does not equate
different wording or establish protection against every adversarial proposal.

Real usefulness and large-graph performance remain unmeasured. The local citation
probe verifies mechanical integrity, and the installed test exercises constrained
MCP selection. Neither proves that prioritizing these labels improves answers.
Retain those limits and finish the broader production work rather than tuning
these twelve cases until reviewers approve.

## Claims rejected against evidence

- Mistral said supporting citations were exposed regardless of scope. The supplied
  collector filters every citation at both endpoints before LIMIT, then rechecks
  through the canonical store. The hidden-edge capacity/count regression and
  installed reclassification test pass. Scoped peer transfer is separate, still
  incomplete work; no transfer readiness was claimed here.
- Mistral described the 20% reserve as showing no measured advantage. It retained
  seven groups against lexical-first's one, while still losing three cases that
  complete-group allocation handled. Improvement and sufficiency are different.
- Qwen called the reserve validated by that improvement. The experiment supports
  a narrow comparison, not a universal 20% threshold or representative quality claim.
- Meta assumed the constructed cases represented typical workloads. The brief
  explicitly denied that. Its broader budget-utilization concern is useful, but
  a four-source group cannot fit a three-source cap without violating the contract.
- The reviewers sometimes treated `semantic_conflict_absence_established:false`
  as a claim of completeness. It explicitly withholds that claim. Wording still
  needs user interpretation testing; a false flag does not certify semantic safety.

## Decision

Preserve the measured comparison and integrate whole-group packing as an evidence
inspection capability with conspicuous omissions. Do not call a proposed label
verified, do not use repetition as authority, and do not describe this as production
acceptance. Finish interpretation review, complete learner ownership and scoped
replica lifecycle before release. The storage decision remains SQLite: this
experiment changes selection, not the relative merits of database engines.
